Fly the Stork VTOL over real Oklahoma farmland. Plan the survey, take off like a quadcopter, transition to wing flight, and cover the field with the Walksnail camera before the battery runs low. The flight model is built from the Stork's measured wing, tail and motor layout.

NEW · RING COURSES

Fly the rings

Thread the Stork through floating rings over the same real fields, in order, then land back on the pad. Every ring and every clip counts against the clock. Turn your sound on: the motors are synthesised live from the flight model.

1 · PlanPick your altitude, line direction, overlap and launch spot. Higher isn't always wider: the fisheye gets too blurry at the edges.
2 · FlyRun the mission on autopilot, or fly it yourself for a 25% bonus. Wind pushes you off the lines; banked turns tilt the camera off the ground.
3 · ScoreStars for coverage at the detail target, battery reserve, beating par time and staying inside the rules: 400 ft ceiling and 1 km line of sight.
Fields: USDA NASS Crop Sequence Boundaries · Imagery: USDA NAIP via USGS · Elevation: USGS 3DEP · Free play imagery: Esri World Imagery.
